## F: Interview Prep — STAR+R Stories

**1. "Walk me through how you handle an ad-hoc analysis request."**
> S: At Moinfinity, a client said "our website traffic is down — figure out why."
> T: Turn vague concern into specific, answerable question.
> A: Scoped question to: "Which acquisition channels dropped and when?" Pulled GA data, segmented by channel/date, identified a UTM tracking break that caused apparent (not real) traffic loss.
> R: Client avoided a panicked campaign spend of ~USD 5k; real issue fixed in 2 hours.
> R: Scoping the question is 50% of ad-hoc analytics.

**2. "Describe your experience with funnel or cohort analysis."**
> S: At Moinfinity, client wanted to improve trial-to-paid conversion for their SaaS tool.
> T: Build a funnel analysis to identify drop-off points.
> A: Mapped user journey stages, built SQL queries on event data, visualised in Power BI with stage conversion rates and time-in-stage.
> R: Identified that 60% of drop-off occurred at "onboarding step 3"; client redesigned that step.
> R: Would do this in BigQuery + Looker at MailerLite.

**3. "How do you help stakeholders ask the right questions?"**
> S: At Mercedes-Benz, management wanted "a report on attendance."
> T: Understand what decision they actually needed to make.
> A: Held a 30-minute scoping session; identified they needed to flag chronic vs. acute absenteeism differently.
> R: Built two-layer report that answered both questions; adopted immediately.
> R: The right question saves 80% of analysis effort.

**4. "Tell me about working with Product Managers on data requests."**
> S: At Moinfinity, a PM-equivalent client asked for "feature adoption data."
> T: Define what "adoption" meant in their context.
> A: Together defined adoption = "used 3+ times in first 30 days"; built cohort view in Power BI.
> R: PM prioritised 2 features for improvement; traffic +200% over 6 months.
> R: Co-defining metrics with PMs is the highest-value analyst activity.

**5. "How do you manage multiple simultaneous analytical requests?"**
> S: At Moinfinity, typically had 3–5 active client deliverables in parallel.
> T: Prioritise, track, and deliver without dropping quality.
> A: Built a personal kanban; classified requests by urgency vs. complexity; batched similar queries.
> R: 7+ clients served simultaneously; zero missed deadlines during 2.5 years.
> R: In a Product + Marketing dual-stakeholder environment, same system applies with a shared tracker.

**6. "Describe a time your analysis changed a marketing decision."**
> S: At Moinfinity, client was spending equally across 4 marketing channels.
> T: Identify which channels drove real conversions vs. vanity traffic.
> A: Built attribution model using UTM data + conversion events; identified 1 channel driving 70% of real leads.
> R: Client reallocated 60% of budget to top channel; lead volume +40% with same spend.
> R: Attribution analysis is the foundation of marketing efficiency.
