## F: Interview Prep — STAR+R Stories

**1. "How do you mine large datasets to surface business insights?"**
> S: At UMPSA, hostel management data was distributed across 5 unlinked systems.
> T: Identify operational trends and surface them to management.
> A: Wrote SQL queries joining all 5 sources; built Python ETL pipeline; created Power BI dashboard with drill-through on problem areas.
> R: Identified 3 key bottlenecks in hostel operations; 75% improvement in notification response time.
> R: In a petabyte-scale environment, would use Spark/Databricks + same analytical mindset.

**2. "Describe designing and monitoring a core business KPI."**
> S: Mercedes-Benz had no standardised attendance KPI; each supervisor measured differently.
> T: Create a single, trusted attendance metric tracked in real time.
> A: Defined KPI formula, built geo-fenced Power Apps data collection, created Power BI monitoring view.
> R: Eliminated 100% of manual calculation; KPI adopted company-wide at the plant.
> R: Would add anomaly detection to flag unusual patterns automatically.

**3. "Tell me about a time you worked with product teams on a data question."**
> S: At Moinfinity, a client's product team wanted to understand which features drove retention.
> T: Translate a vague business question into a specific analytical framework.
> A: Defined the event model, built a funnel analysis in web analytics tools, surfaced cohort comparison.
> R: Product team made 2 feature prioritisation decisions based on the analysis; traffic +200%.
> R: Would formalise this into a product analytics model in a Sporty Group context.

**4. "How do you handle 'complete freedom on methodology' — how do you structure your own analysis?"**
> S: At Moinfinity, every client had different data and different questions.
> T: Deliver consistent quality without a prescribed framework.
> A: Developed a personal analytical template: (1) business question → (2) data source mapping → (3) hypothesis → (4) analysis → (5) confidence-rated insight.
> R: 7+ successful client engagements; all delivered measurable outcomes.
> R: This discipline becomes even more valuable at scale.

**5. "What's your approach to A/B testing or experimental analysis?"**
> S: At Moinfinity, a client ran two landing page variants simultaneously.
> T: Determine which version drove more conversions with statistical confidence.
> A: Set up tracking, defined sample size requirements, ran chi-square test on conversion rates.
> R: Recommended Variant B with 92% confidence; client adopted it; conversion improved 18%.
> R: Would formalise A/B testing governance in a sports betting context where decision speed matters.

**6. "How do you deliver reports to executives with very different priorities?"**
> S: At Mercedes-Benz, VP and plant managers had different views on what mattered most.
> T: Build one dashboard that serves multiple executive audiences.
> A: Built layered Power BI dashboard — executive summary on page 1, operational drill-through on pages 2–3.
> R: VP praised the system; managers used it daily for operational decisions.
> R: Separation of strategic and operational views is key to executive adoption.
