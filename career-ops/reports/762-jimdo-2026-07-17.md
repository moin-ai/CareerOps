While the underlying concepts transfer (SQL is SQL; Python is Python), the specific platform experience and certifications do not align. Additionally, Glassdoor data suggests Jimdo pays Data Engineers approximately €53,000–€57,000 — which is below Moin's stated target range and represents weak comp for a DE role with this tech stack.
