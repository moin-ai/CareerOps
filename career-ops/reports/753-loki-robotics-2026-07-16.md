Loki Robotics is a Zürich-based startup building cleaning robots for commercial restrooms and kitchens. They raised $1.6M pre-seed. The role owns the software layer connecting robots to operators: telemetry ingestion, command APIs, fleet dashboard (customer-facing), mission control UI, internal tooling for engineers. On-site in Zürich is mandatory. Start date: ASAP.

**Switzerland visa context:** Switzerland is NOT an EU member and does not participate in the EU Blue Card scheme. For non-EU/EFTA nationals, the B permit is quota-based with annual federal limits. Employers must demonstrate no suitable EU/Swiss candidate was available (labour market test). Processing: 1–3 months. This is significantly more complex and uncertain than Germany's EU Blue Card route.
