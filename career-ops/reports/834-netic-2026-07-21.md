**R:** 40–60% reduction in issue response times; 1–2 hours saved daily at the assembly line; 100% process transparency
**R:** Real-world customer co-creation — not a spec handed down from above — is what makes software actually get adopted

### F3 — Founder-Level Ownership Under Ambiguity (Moinfinity Digital)
**S:** Had no employer, no mentor, no office — just client needs and a commitment to deliver
**T:** Independently scope, staff, build, and ship digital products for clients in the US, Malaysia, and Australia while still enrolled in university

**R:** 40% operational efficiency improvement for clients; 200%+ web traffic increase; 2–6 hours daily manual effort saved; built lasting partnerships across 3 countries

### F4 — Explaining Technical Trade-offs to Non-Technical Executives (Mercedes-Benz)
**S:** Stakeholders would request features that were technically infeasible or architecturally unsound within the Microsoft Power Platform environment
**T:** Redirect without undermining confidence, propose alternatives that still delivered business value

**R:** Recognized for consulting excellence by VP and plant management; trusted to interview candidates as a technical evaluator within 5 months

### F5 — Shipping Automation with Real Business Impact (Power Automate Data Flow)
**S:** An internal system was generating data that needed manual extraction, structuring, and filing — consuming hours per week
**T:** Automate the full flow: receive, parse, structure, store

**R:** Eliminated recurring manual effort; data was always current and accessible; enabled downstream reporting

**S:** Production KPIs (daily working hours, target vs. actual, trend lines) were tracked manually in spreadsheets with no reliable view for management
