## F: Interview Prep (STAR+R Stories)

**1. "Tell me about a data platform you built from the ground up"**
> Mercedes-Benz attendance system: no prior data infrastructure existed. Started with stakeholder interviews to understand reporting needs, designed data model (fact: attendance events; dims: employees, shifts, locations), built Power BI layer on top. Result: 100% manual elimination, used daily by 500+ staff.

**2. "How do you translate vague business requirements into a data product?"**
> UMPSA hostel management: stakeholders said "we need better data." Ran 3 discovery workshops, identified 5 core metrics (occupancy rate, late payments, maintenance backlog, etc.). Built automated pipeline + dashboard. Reduced reporting from 5h → 30min.

**3. "What's your approach to data quality and trust?"**
> Both UMPSA and Moinfinity: validation at ingestion (null checks, range validation, duplicate detection), alerts for anomalies, documentation of data lineage. Trust is earned through consistency — when a dashboard shows clean data week after week, stakeholders start making decisions from it.

**4. "Describe a SQL challenge involving large-scale data"**
> Power BI with 500+ employee attendance data across 6 months: DAX measures calculating running totals across date hierarchies caused performance issues. Redesigned data model to pre-aggregate at daily level, reducing query time from 12s → under 2s.

**5. "How do you work with non-technical stakeholders?"**
> Mercedes-Benz: VP Andreas Lettner used my dashboards for plant-level decisions. I ran bi-weekly review sessions, translated metric questions into data model changes, and documented every field so any business user could self-serve. Trusted to interview data candidates within 5 months.

**6. "Why analytics engineering vs. data science?"**
> I care about the infrastructure that enables decisions — not just the decisions themselves. Analytics engineering is where I can have the most leverage: build once, enable hundreds of stakeholders. Data science without reliable data engineering is an illusion.

**7. "Why Meister?"**
> Productivity tools are about understanding human behavior at scale — how people collaborate, what workflows succeed. Building analytics on top of that data to help Meister's product team make better decisions is exactly the kind of meaningful work I want to do.
