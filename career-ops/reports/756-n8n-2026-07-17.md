n8n is a Berlin-founded, open-source workflow automation platform backed by Sequoia Capital and Accel with 150+ employees. They are remote-first with a center of gravity in Berlin and explicitly confirm they can sponsor visas to Germany. The product competes with Zapier and Make in the automation space and has strong PLG motion — most revenue is self-serve.

The role is squarely focused on owning the PLG funnel: activation → monetization → retention → expansion analytics. The analyst owns PQL models, funnel mapping, A/B test design, and cross-functional influence on Product, Marketing, and Engineering. The tech stack is BigQuery, dbt, Metabase, Google Sheets, and Python for ad-hoc analysis. This is product analytics in a SaaS company — not BI/reporting in an operations context.

The key difficulty for Moin: n8n wants someone who has already lived inside a PLG SaaS company and understands behavioral product data at scale. Moin's entire analytics background is in manufacturing operational reporting (Mercedes-Benz KPI dashboards, Power BI production tracking). The domain, toolchain, and data type are all different.
