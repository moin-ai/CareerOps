## Block F — STAR+R Interview Stories

### Story 1 — Production KPI Dashboard at Fortune 500
**Requirement:** Deliver insightful analysis with actionable recommendations that drive business impact.
**S:** Mercedes-Benz Malaysia plant had manual daily attendance and production calculations — error-prone and time-consuming for plant managers.
**T:** Design and build a production analytics solution with real-time KPIs.
**A:** Architected Power BI dashboards with DAX measures tracking daily working hours, target vs. actual, trend lines. Integrated automated daily refresh from SharePoint Online. Eliminated 100% of manual calculations.
**R:** Plant managers had instant, reliable KPI views. VP Andreas Lettner and General Managers recognized the impact on production efficiency.
**R (reflection):** "The insight was that production staff weren't making bad decisions — they were making slow ones because the data was hard to access. Making it instant changed behavior."

### Story 2 — Escalation Analysis Driving 40-60% Response Time Reduction
**Requirement:** Identify high-value opportunities to unlock business potential.
**S:** Assembly line at Mercedes-Benz had no structured escalation data. Issues were tracked informally, response times unknown.
**T:** Build a system to capture, track, and analyze fault escalations.
**A:** Developed a fault-escalation management system with mobile app and real-time tracking. Analyzed escalation patterns to identify bottlenecks. Data showed 1–2 hours were being lost daily at assembly due to unresolved faults.
**R:** Response times dropped 40–60%. 100% process transparency enabled data-driven staffing and prioritization decisions.
**R:** "This is when I learned that the most valuable analysis isn't always complex — it's the one that makes the invisible visible."

### Story 3 — 5h → 30min Reporting Automation at University
**Requirement:** Data analytics delivering measurable business impact on operational workflows.
**S:** UMPSA administrative staff spent 5 hours compiling hostel management reports manually, prone to errors and delays.
**T:** Automate the reporting pipeline to reduce burden and improve decision-making speed for administrators.
**A:** Analyzed existing data flows, redesigned the reporting architecture, built automated workflows reducing compilation time and improving data accuracy.
**R:** 5 hours → 30 minutes (90% reduction). Administrators could make decisions faster and with higher confidence in the data.
**R:** "Process automation without data quality is just fast garbage. I made sure the output was trusted before I made it automatic."

### Story 4 — C-Level Analytics Partnership (Moinfinity Digital)
**Requirement:** Partner with business leaders to deeply understand challenges and drive strategic decisions.
**S:** A client at Moinfinity (corporate training firm, Australia) had web and engagement data but no structured analytics to understand where traffic was converting.
**T:** Conduct discovery, analyze existing data, deliver actionable growth recommendations.
**A:** Led client discovery workshops, analyzed user behavior and funnel data, identified key conversion drop-off points, delivered prioritized recommendations with projected impact.
**R:** Client implemented top recommendations; web traffic increased 200%+. Moin maintained the client relationship through the full delivery lifecycle.
**R:** "The best analysis is useless without executive buy-in. I learned to frame findings in business terms — not metrics, but decisions."
