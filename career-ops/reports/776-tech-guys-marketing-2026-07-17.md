Tech Guys Who Get Marketing is a small US boutique consultancy that bridges technology and marketing for Fortune 500 companies, private businesses, and nonprofits. The team culture is described as "motley crew of misfits" with an entrepreneur-friendly ethos. The Data Engineer role is a freelance contractor position (1099) at $50–75/hr for 25–30 hours/week.

The technical requirements are a reasonable fit: Python, SQL, and data warehouses (Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery). Moin's Azure Databricks cert and Python/SQL background align well. The marketing data domain differs from Moin's analytics/ops background, but the consulting delivery model closely mirrors his Moinfinity Digital experience.

**The critical issue is timezone:** The company explicitly lists accepted timezones as EST, CST, MST, PST, AKST, and HST — all US (and Hawaii) timezones. Bangladesh is UTC+6, which is 10.5–16.5 hours ahead of these US timezones. There is no indication of asynchronous or flexible timezone accommodation. While realworkfromanywhere.com lists the company, the timezone restriction overrides that label for practical purposes.

**Additional concern:** The 1099 form is a US tax document for US-based contractors. International contractors would typically receive a W-8BEN arrangement. The 1099 mention signals the company may be targeting US residents. The application process (video + resume; "resumes without videos will be ignored") is consistent with a small, informal US company.
