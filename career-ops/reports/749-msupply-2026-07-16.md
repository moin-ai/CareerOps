**Plan "vender senior sin mentir":** Not applicable. Attempting to position entry-level management experience (none) as equivalent to 3+ years of leading technical teams would be misleading and counterproductive. A recruiter would identify the gap immediately.

**If the candidate wants to target this company in the future:** Apply for a Data Engineer or BI Developer role — mSupply appears to have multiple data-adjacent openings — and grow into management over 2–3 years.
