**1. "Tell me about a time you built a dashboard that changed a business decision."**
> S: Mercedes-Benz Malaysia attendance was tracked manually across 1,200+ workers.
> T: Build a Power BI system replacing all manual calculations.
> A: Designed geo-fenced Power Apps check-in feeding into Power BI attendance reports.
> R: Eliminated 100% manual effort; VP Andreas Lettner praised the system publicly.
> R (reflection): Would next add predictive absenteeism flagging.

**2. "How do you identify what 'great' looks like for operational performance?"**
> S: At UMPSA hostel, no standard existed for defining good vs. poor resident notification response.
> T: Establish a baseline and surface insights to admin.
> A: Analysed historical response data, defined quartile benchmarks, built tracking.
> R: 75% improvement in notification response time.
> R: Would tie to SLAs in a commercial product context.

**3. "Describe a time you had to balance speed vs. accuracy in an analysis."**
> S: Escalation management at Mercedes-Benz required fast triaging but also root cause accuracy.
> T: Needed a system that flagged quickly AND told managers why.
> A: Built two-layer Power BI view — live triage dashboard + weekly deep-dive report.
> R: 40–60% reduction in issue response time; deeper analysis ran post-incident.
> R: Learned to document "confidence level" of each insight.

**4. "How do you partner with PMs who aren't data-savvy?"**
> S: At Moinfinity, clients had limited data literacy; they wanted "just a number."
> T: Translate complex funnel analysis into actionable next steps.
> A: Introduced a simple "traffic light" summary page in all reports + verbal walkthrough.
> R: 200%+ web traffic increase for clients; decisions made faster.
> R: Strong communication beats perfect analysis every time.

**5. "Tell me about a time you improved data quality."**
> S: UMPSA hostel data had duplicate entries and inconsistent formats across departments.
> T: Unify data into a single clean model.
> A: Built ETL process in Python to standardise, deduplicate, and load into a central table.
> R: Reduced compilation time from 5h to 30min; zero data disputes post-launch.
> R: Would add automated schema validation earlier in the pipeline.

**6. "Give an example of creating venue or customer archetypes/segments."**
> S: Moinfinity consulting — clients across US, Malaysia, Australia had very different usage patterns.
> T: Identify which client profiles benefited most from which service packages.
> A: Segmented by industry vertical + engagement depth; matched service offerings accordingly.
> R: Improved onboarding fit; 40% efficiency improvement in ops delivery.
> R: Apply similar segmentation logic to ROLLER venue "shapes."
