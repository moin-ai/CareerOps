Headquartered in Cologne, Germany, they own the digital product and data platform for Eurowings airline operations and passenger-facing services. Being part of the Lufthansa Group provides financial stability and enterprise-scale infrastructure. The team is remote-first within Germany with low office visit cadence (1–2x monthly).

The Senior Data Engineer role sits on the Data Engineering team, building and maintaining cloud-based data pipelines on Azure and Databricks. Responsibilities include designing ETL/ELT systems, contributing to the Azure+Databricks data platform, integrating streaming (Kafka/Event Hubs), DataOps CI/CD, and collaborating with analysts and data scientists. The tech stack requires Scala or Python, Spark in production, Azure/Databricks, and streaming frameworks.

One genuine bright spot: the role specifically calls out Azure Databricks, and Moin holds an Azure Databricks Platform Architect certification (2026) — the most relevant technical signal in his profile for data engineering roles. However, the 7+ year experience requirement and the requirement for production-grade Scala or Kafka work represent hard blockers.
