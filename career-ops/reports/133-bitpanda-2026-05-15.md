## F: Interview Prep (STAR+R Stories)

**1. "Tell me about a complex BI system you built end-to-end"**
> Situation: Mercedes-Benz needed real-time attendance tracking across a plant floor. Task: Build a geo-fenced Power Apps + Power BI system. Action: Designed data model, DAX measures, Power Automate flows; eliminated manual spreadsheets. Result: 100% manual calculation eliminated; used by 500+ employees. Reflection: Would add automated data quality checks (like dbt tests) in retrospect.

**2. "How have you worked with Finance or Operations stakeholders?"**
> Mercedes-Benz Ops team: weekly alignment sessions with department heads, VP Andreas Lettner reviewed deliverables personally. Translated vague requests ("we need to track attendance better") into a defined data model with clear KPIs.

**3. "Describe a time you improved data quality"**
> UMPSA hostel system: discovered duplicate records and inconsistent entries across 5 hours of manual Excel compilation. Built automated data validation in the system, reducing compilation time from 5h → 30min and eliminating reconciliation errors.

**4. "How do you enable self-service analytics across an organization?"**
> Mercedes-Benz: trained 8 department managers on Power BI dashboards; created documentation and tooltips inside the report; set up row-level security so each manager saw only their team's data. Adoption reached 90%+ within 4 weeks.

**5. "Tell me about a time you worked with incomplete or messy data"**
> Moinfinity client: inherited poorly structured CRM exports with inconsistent date formats and duplicates. Used Python/Pandas to clean, standardize, and load into a reporting pipeline. Delivered clean weekly KPI dashboard within 2 weeks.

**6. "How do you balance engineering rigor with business speed?"**
> Mercedes-Benz escalation system: initial version shipped in 2 weeks (MVP), then iterated with feedback. Prioritized working system over perfect architecture first — reduced issue response time 40–60% immediately. Refactored for scale in month 2.

**7. "What's your experience with data modeling?"**
> Designed star-schema-equivalent data models in Power BI (fact tables for attendance events, dimension tables for employees/departments/locations). Understand dimensional modeling principles and apply them in Power BI and SQL contexts.

**8. "Why FinTech / why Bitpanda?"**
> Financial data has real stakes — errors affect people's money. My passion for clean, trustworthy data pipelines aligns with that. Crypto/investing is where BI enables fast product decisions at scale — exactly the environment where I want to apply these skills.
